## Rulings

**1. The doctrine risk was overstated.** The ledger quarantined the three entries at intake —
*"treat these three as observational only — do NOT advance a counter on them"* — and posed two
questions to the Consul. That containment is correct. No intervention required. A Chronicler's
concern that doctrine is "accruing under something with no constitutional standing" does not
survive reading what the ledger actually says.

**2. The register is the defect.** `data/volumes.json` is Codex's data layer: Cluster A, my
jurisdiction. The ledger records that `soma-internal` *"runs its own Builder + four-Governor
model"* and is *"the operational codex behind Cluster B's invoicing repo."* An operational
Province with a seated Builder is absent from the Republic's own Province register.

This is the April failure repeating. On 2026-04-20 seven Capital-native companions were found
inscribed in the Constitution and invisible to `companions.json`; stubs were created to reconcile
the archive to the roster. The same class of blindness, in the same repository, four months later
— and this time it was not caught by a reconciliation pass but by a doctrine-ledger footnote.

**3. I decline the cluster question.** If `soma-internal` sits in Cluster B it is Nyx's
censorial ground. The ledger routed it to the Consul correctly. A Censor of Cluster A ruling on
Cluster B membership would be the jurisdictional overreach canon-inst-003 was careful to avoid
when it named my Cluster C authority *interim* rather than absorbing the cluster outright.

**4. A four-Governor model is unexplained by the cascade.** Book III Article 2 triggers one
Governor at 30,000 LOC. Four Governors implies either mass the register does not record, or a
governance shape the Constitution does not describe. This bears directly on the Book III Article
2 amendment now under Sovereign review: the cascade already fails to describe live practice, and
this is a second instance beyond MyFin. Whoever holds that amendment should know it.

**5. Decree 0020 may be incomplete.** The Cluster table ratified 2026-08-26 names Cluster B as
SEP Invoicing + SEP Dashboard. If `soma-internal` belongs there, the table is behind as of
ratification. This is not a defect in the decree — the decree could not know — but it should be
recorded so the table's next amendment is not mistaken for a correction of sloppiness.
