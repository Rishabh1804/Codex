Executive summary

Where the tournament stands, in 30 seconds.

The first-ever 32-team knockout stage has already produced history. Germany — four-time champions — were dumped out by Paraguay on penalties (1–1, 4–3) in what's being called the biggest knockout upset ever and Germany's first-ever World Cup shootout defeat. Hours later the Netherlands went the same way, beaten 3–2 on spot-kicks by a fearless Morocco.

Earlier, Canada beat South Africa 1–0 and Brazil survived a stoppage-time scare to edge Japan 2–1. That makes four into the Round of 16: Canada, Brazil, Paraguay and Morocco — with Morocco now facing co-hosts Canada.

Still to come are the heavyweight openers: France–Sweden, Spain–Austria, Portugal–Croatia, Argentina–Cape Verde, USA–Bosnia and England–DR Congo.
